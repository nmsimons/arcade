Privacy policy

This policy covers Dream Large Arcade (“Arcade”), its browser games, level tools, and optional account and cloud-save features. The account consent screen and cloud folders use the name Dream Large Arcade.

You can play without signing in. Anonymous saves and personal bests stay in your browser. Local level folders stay on your device.

Desktop downloads

The downloads page checks GitHub's public releases API for available desktop downloads. This request does not include Arcade saves, account details, or authentication credentials. GitHub receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address. Download links also lead to GitHub, which processes requests under its privacy statement. Desktop game progress is saved locally on your computer, separately from browser progress; desktop cloud sync is not available yet.

Optional accounts and cloud storage

Signing in with Google or a personal Microsoft account creates a separate save space on this device. Your provider returns an account identifier and basic profile information such as your name and email address. Arcade uses the identifier to separate saves and your name, or email address when a name is unavailable, as the displayed account label. Signing in does not automatically copy anonymous progress.

Enabling cloud storage is a separate choice. Google Drive access covers files created or selected through this app, using a visible Dream Large Arcade folder in My Drive. It does not grant access to your whole Drive or automatically to every file placed in that folder elsewhere. OneDrive access is limited to this app's folder, normally Apps/Dream Large Arcade; its name comes from the Microsoft app registration. The account panel links directly to your cloud folder. Game data is organized in folders per game; custom levels are ordinary JSON files with a collection index. Cloud files contain game saves, custom levels, personal bests and version metadata. They are not public leaderboards.

Your browser sends this data directly to your storage provider. Arcade has no account database, save upload service or server token store. Arcade remembers your selected provider, account identifier, display name and cloud-save choice in persistent browser storage so you stay signed in across visits until you sign out or clear site data. Google's short-lived access token and expiry are held only in tab session storage; refreshes can reuse unexpired access, while closing the tab or access expiring requires an explicit Reconnect to sync again. Microsoft's sign-in library stores authentication tokens and account details in its local-storage cache. Microsoft normally encrypts those credentials with a key that expires when the browser closes, unless you select “Keep me signed in” during Microsoft sign-in. Expired cloud credentials leave your selected Arcade profile and local saves available. Sign-out clears the remembered account and its cached credentials; disconnect stops syncing and clears Google's cached access. Google and Microsoft process sign-in and storage requests under their own privacy policies. The site host may retain ordinary request logs such as IP addresses and requested paths; this page does not promise that hosting providers keep no logs.

How data is used and shared

Arcade uses account information and provider access to identify your save space, display your account, and provide the cloud saves and file selection you enable. Arcade does not sell this information or use your Google or Microsoft data for advertising. Files are sent to the storage provider you connect to provide these features.

If you choose to export and share a level or backup, the recipients can access the information in that file. If you email support, the maintainer receives your email address and the information you include and uses it to respond to your request. Your email provider and the maintainer's email provider also process the message. Do not send passwords or access tokens. Include game files only when needed for your request and when you are comfortable sharing their contents.

Control and recovery

  • Changes save locally first and sync in the background, including during play. Incoming saves wait for a safe menu or an explicit refresh or restore. Wait for sync to finish before closing the browser.
  • Each cloud sync that changes data creates a version. Conflicting versions are retained until you choose which progress to continue.
  • Sign-out clears the app's credentials and returns to anonymous play. It keeps your local and cloud saves. Browser caches are not encrypted private vaults; someone using the same browser profile may access them.
  • Disconnecting stops cloud sync. To revoke the app's grant, use your provider's permissions settings. This is separate from signing out.
  • Use Manage saves → Download backup to keep a portable JSON backup. You can restore that backup through Import backup.
  • Choose from Google Drive grants Arcade access to the individual files you select. Levels already in the Arcade Levels folder sync in place; levels selected elsewhere are copied into it, leaving the originals intact. Files added to Drive later need to be selected separately.
  • Clear the site's browser data to remove local copies. To remove cloud copies, delete the Arcade folder in OneDrive or Google Drive. Any hidden Google app data from an older version can be removed through Drive's Manage apps settings. Disconnect first so future syncs do not recreate data.

Files count toward your storage quota. Arcade retains cloud versions and does not automatically delete older saves. Revoking permission alone does not necessarily delete data.
